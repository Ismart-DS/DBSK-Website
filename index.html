Welcome to Dan Bahadur Surya Kumar Inter College

Welcome to Dan Bahadur Surya Kumar Inter College, a place where learning meets inspiration and every child gets the opportunity to discover their true potential.

We are committed to providing quality education in a supportive and disciplined environment. Our focus is not only on academic excellence but also on developing confidence, creativity, moral values and leadership qualities among students.

With dedicated educators, modern learning practices and a student-centred approach, we strive to prepare our students for a successful and meaningful future.
